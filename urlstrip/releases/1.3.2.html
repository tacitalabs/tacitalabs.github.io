Desktop release notes

URLStrip 1.3.2 (Build 28)

Clean Amazon app share links without carrying their tracking data forward.

What changed

  • Expands supported a.co links shared by the Amazon app into canonical Amazon product URLs.
  • Removes Amazon share and referral parameters while preserving the exact product ASIN.
  • Supports both explicit cleaning and opt-in automatic resolution on desktop.
  • Rejects lookalike hosts, malformed tokens, unsupported destinations, credentials, and non-default ports.
  • Keeps the original short link unchanged when resolution fails or the destination is not an eligible Amazon product.

Privacy and network behavior

Normal URL cleaning stays local. Resolving a supported Amazon short link requires a bounded network request to a.co. Automatic resolution remains opt-in. Choosing Clean Clipboard Now is an explicit request to resolve an eligible short link.
