Start with the record, not the form

Data broker removals fail in predictable ways. People choose a similar name, lose the listing URL, miss an email confirmation, or assume that a submitted form means the public record disappeared.

These guides take the slower and more reliable route:

  1. Identify the exact record.
  2. Save the listing and review what it exposes.
  3. Use the broker's own removal process.
  4. Complete the email, phone, CAPTCHA, or final-review step.
  5. Return after the broker's processing window and check again.

That is also how Infobreaker works. It keeps the profile, evidence, and monitoring history on your computer and sends information directly to the broker only when you choose to scan or submit a request.

Broker sites change constantly. If a form, requirement, or confirmation differs from the guide, stop and follow the broker's current instructions. Do not force a request through a page you cannot confidently identify.

What these guides do not promise

No guide can guarantee that a broker will accept a request, remove every record, or keep a listing down permanently. Some brokers require more identity information. Some decline requests. Some records return.

Infobreaker preserves those outcomes rather than flattening them into a single success percentage. It is a public beta and is not an emergency safety, legal, or guaranteed-removal service.
