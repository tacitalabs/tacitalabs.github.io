Check the phone and location, not only the name

USPhoneBook records can look convincing because a name matches while the phone or city belongs to someone else. Review all visible details and open the individual record before starting removal.

Save the profile URL and the phone number shown on it. Those details make the later verification much less ambiguous.

How the USPhoneBook opt-out process works

  1. Search for your name and review the matching record.
  2. Confirm the location and phone details belong to you.
  3. Open the official USPhoneBook opt-out page.
  4. Follow the broker's prompts for the selected record.
  5. Complete any CAPTCHA or browser challenge.
  6. Open the email link USPhoneBook sends.
  7. Finish the linked removal form and save the confirmation.
  8. Revisit the original record and search again later.

The first form requests an email link. It does not by itself prove the listing was removed.

How Infobreaker helps with USPhoneBook

Infobreaker can search by name, compare the result with the local profile, and present the exact record for review. The assisted removal opens USPhoneBook's own form and pauses for the challenge and email steps.

Afterward, Infobreaker can check the saved profile and record whether it is still present, gone, or inconclusive. It can also schedule another check because phone-directory listings may return.

Common USPhoneBook removal problems

The search opens a page with no matching card

Search using a name variation or a known city. Do not treat an empty or unexpected page as proof that an older saved record is gone.

I received the email but did not finish the linked form

Open the current email link and complete the broker's instructions. If the link expired, restart the request against the same confirmed record.

The phone number is gone but the name still appears

Open the remaining record and compare its URL and location with your original evidence. It may be a partial record, a second listing, or a different person.

THE HONEST LINE

A request is not a removal.

Infobreaker records the request, preserves the evidence, and schedules a later check. It reports a verified removal only when that check shows the public listing is gone. Broker delays, declines, and re-listings remain visible instead of being folded into a success number.
