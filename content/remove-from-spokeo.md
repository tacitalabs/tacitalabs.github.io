## Collect the public listing URLs first

Spokeo can publish more than one record for the same person. Name variations,
old cities, and partial records may lead to separate profile URLs. One request
may hide only the listing you submit.

Infobreaker does not currently treat Spokeo's paid search funnel as a reliable
discovery scan. Locate the public listing through Spokeo or a normal web
search, confirm it is yours, and save the exact URL before opening the opt-out
form.

## How the Spokeo opt-out process works

1. **Find every distinct Spokeo profile that belongs to you.**
2. **Save each profile URL and the search variation that found it.**
3. **Open Spokeo's official opt-out form.**
4. **Paste one profile URL and enter an email address you control.**
5. **Complete the CAPTCHA and submit the form.**
6. **Open the confirmation email.**
7. **Repeat the process for each separate listing URL.**
8. **Search all known name and location variations again after processing.**

> Do not pay for a background report just to reach the opt-out form. The
> removal route takes a public listing URL and email address directly.

## How Infobreaker helps with Spokeo

When you have a confirmed Spokeo listing, Infobreaker can open the opt-out
route, carry the exact profile URL into the request, and pause for the CAPTCHA
and email link. It keeps separate requests tied to separate records so one
successful confirmation does not erase other known exposures from the work
list.

The app can also schedule verification against the saved URLs. A listing that
disappears can be recorded as verified removed; another that remains stays
visible as unfinished work.

## Common Spokeo removal problems

### I cannot see enough detail without paying

Use only the public information you can safely confirm. If the record is
ambiguous, do not submit it as yours.

### I removed one profile but another search still finds me

Compare the URLs. A separate profile usually needs a separate request and
email confirmation.

### I submitted the form but did not receive an email

Check spam and confirm the address was entered correctly. If necessary, restart
the request for that exact URL and preserve the new receipt.
