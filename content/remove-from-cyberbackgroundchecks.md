## Treat this as a two-stage request

CyberBackgroundChecks can expose a person record built from names, locations,
addresses, and possible associates. Confirm the exact profile before you begin.

The removal flow does not end with the first form. That step asks the broker to
send a final identity form by email. You still need to open the link and finish
the broker's instructions.

## How the CyberBackgroundChecks removal process works

1. **Search for your name and open the matching person record.**
2. **Compare the location and identity details carefully.**
3. **Open the official removal page.**
4. **Submit the initial name and email request for the selected record.**
5. **Complete any Cloudflare or CAPTCHA challenge in the same browser.**
6. **Open the email CyberBackgroundChecks sends.**
7. **Complete the final identity form and save its confirmation.**
8. **Recheck the public person record after processing.**

> “Check your email” means the request is waiting on you. It is not a completed
> removal and should not start the verification clock yet.

## How Infobreaker helps with CyberBackgroundChecks

Infobreaker can scan the site, keep the matched record attached to the
removal, and prepare the broker's initial form. It leaves the assisted browser
open if a challenge appears so you can solve it without losing the record
context.

When the broker sends its link, you complete the final identity form. Only
after that does Infobreaker schedule the later record check.

## Common CyberBackgroundChecks removal problems

### The browser challenge cleared the email field

Review and refill the visible form before submission. A solved challenge does
not guarantee that earlier field values survived.

### I received the broker email but stopped there

Open the link and complete the final form. Preserve the first email and the
final confirmation as separate pieces of evidence.

### The public page redirects or changes shape

Search again from the broker's main people-search path. A redirect alone is not
enough to prove removal; it can also indicate a site change or a moved record.
