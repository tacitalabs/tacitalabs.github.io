## Prepare the information the form requires

ThatsThem can connect a person's name to addresses, phone numbers, email
addresses, and related records. Its current first-party removal form may ask
for your full name, street address, city, state, ZIP code, phone number, and
email address.

That is sensitive information. Use only the broker's official form, verify the
domain before typing, and review what will be sent.

## How the ThatsThem opt-out process works

1. **Search by name and location.**
2. **Open the matching record and confirm the address details.**
3. **Prepare the full address, ZIP code, phone number, and email address the
   removal form requires.**
4. **Open ThatsThem's official opt-out form.**
5. **Review each filled field against the listing and your current details.**
6. **Solve the Turnstile challenge in the visible browser.**
7. **Submit and save the explicit confirmation.**
8. **Check your email for any broker follow-up and revisit the record later.**

> Do not fill a missing phone number or address with invented data. If the
> broker requires information you cannot safely provide, stop and use its
> current privacy contact for guidance.

Infobreaker can scan ThatsThem, show the matching result, and prepare the

final review and Turnstile challenge.

The app captures the submitted state only when the broker shows a recognizable
confirmation. It then schedules a recheck against the saved listing instead of
counting the form submission as a removal.

## Common ThatsThem removal problems

### The form says required information is missing

Check street address, ZIP code, phone number, and email. The current flow
requires all of them, even when the public result shows only part of the data.

### Turnstile reloads the page

Review the form again. A browser challenge can clear or change field values.
Submit only after the visible values remain correct.

### The confirmation says to check email

Keep the email with the request evidence and complete any final broker step.
The public listing still needs a separate later check.
