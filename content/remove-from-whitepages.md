## What Whitepages may expose

A Whitepages result can connect a name to current or previous addresses, phone
numbers, age ranges, and possible relatives. That combination is what makes a
listing useful to someone trying to identify where you live or who is close to
you.

Start by checking the full record, not only the name. A matching city, former
address, phone number, or relative can help distinguish your listing from
someone with a similar name.

## How the Whitepages removal process works

1. **Save the exact listing URL.** You need the person record, not a name-search
   results page.
2. **Open the official suppression request.** Paste the saved listing and
   confirm that Whitepages selected the same person you reviewed.
3. **Choose a reason and review the request.** Do not submit against a weak or
   ambiguous match.
4. **Complete the verification step.** The standard route may use a phone
   callback. Whitepages also handles privacy-rights requests through its
   privacy process when the callback route is not practical.
5. **Keep the confirmation.** It proves the request was accepted, not that the
   listing has already disappeared.
6. **Check the original record later.** Search variations and a direct visit to
   the saved URL help catch partial removals or a listing that returned.

> A phone call, acknowledgement, or request number is an intermediate state.
> Do not publish or share the verification code Whitepages gives you.

## Common Whitepages removal problems

### The phone callback is not practical

Use the broker's current privacy-request instructions instead of inventing a
phone number or asking another person to impersonate you. The request may take
longer, but the identity trail stays honest.

### The name disappeared but the address still appears

Search by phone number, address, and known name variations. A broker can hold
more than one public record for the same person.

### Whitepages says the request was accepted

Save the receipt and wait for processing. Acceptance is not the same as a
verified removal.
